Website Privacy Notice

Last Updated: October 2026 · Operated by Jarrod Avery d/b/a Supply4u

At Supply4u, we believe in privacy by design. We build privacy-preserving software for our clients and uphold the same standards on our own website. This Privacy Notice explains how information is handled when you visit www.supply4uapps.com or submit a project inquiry.

1. Information We Collect

When you voluntarily submit a project inquiry through our website form, project estimator, or direct email, we collect the contact details and project specifications you provide, including:

  • Your name and email address
  • Target platforms (iOS, Android, Web) and estimated budget parameters
  • Your project description, features, and timeline goals

We do not use invasive third-party tracking pixels, keystroke recorders, or cross-site advertising networks on this site.

2. How We Use Project Information

Any information submitted via our project calculator or inquiry form is used strictly to:

  • Evaluate your technical requirements and formulate a milestone-based proposal and quote.
  • Correspond with you directly via email regarding your project.
  • Establish development agreements and deliverable roadmaps.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or proprietary project ideas to third parties or brokers.

3. Product Privacy (SlipStash & Serenity)

Our individual shipped software applications have dedicated on-device and product privacy policies:

  • SlipStash: Operates 100% locally on-device. Receipts, OCR scans, and purchase records never leave your physical phone and are never transmitted to cloud servers.
  • Serenity: Local reflections and mood entries are stored privately on your device. Any optional live AI interactions are strictly opt-in and processed in isolated sessions.

4. Confidentiality of Client Ideas

We treat all client concepts, wireframes, and business ideas submitted to Supply4u with strict professional confidentiality. If your organization requires a formal Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) prior to detailed scoping, please let us know.

Questions or Data Inquiries?

For privacy questions, corrections, or deletion requests, contact Jarrod Avery directly at info@supply4uapps.com.
